Our story

From a Family Kitchen to a Local Favorite

Mama G began as a small family kitchen in Makurdi, where a mother, her recipes, and a love for welcoming people shaped every meal. She cooked for neighbors, friends, and family members who kept returning for the same comforting taste and generous spirit.

As word spread, more people came to enjoy her jollof rice, pepper soup, pounded yam, and other traditional dishes. What started as a simple act of sharing food grew into a warm and vibrant Nigerian restaurant dedicated to preserving authentic flavors while creating a modern dining experience.

From the first chopping of ingredients to the last garnish, every dish is made with care and attention to detail.
